Zebrino Privacy Policy

Effective date: 4 October 2026

Zebrino puts your barcode cards (loyalty, library, gym, membership) on your Zepp OS watch. In short: your cards stay on your phone and your watch. No servers, no account, no ads, no tracking.

What Zebrino stores

  • Your cards: name, number, barcode type and value, colour, and the optional note, balance and expiry date you add.
  • Your settings: brightness, card order, suggestions on or off.
  • On the watch only: cards ready to display, recently used cards and, if suggestions are on, the times of day you usually show each card.

Zebrino does not collect your name, e-mail, location, contacts, health data or any advertising identifier.

Where it is kept

On your phone, inside the Zepp app, and on your watch. Cards travel only between the two, through the Zepp app's own connection. Zebrino never sends data to its developer or anyone else and does not use the internet. The Zepp app itself follows Zepp Health's privacy policy.

What Zebrino never does

No accounts, no ads, no analytics, no sale or sharing of data, no third-party SDKs. Its open-source libraries (bwip-js, ZML) run on your devices and send nothing.

Permissions

Only local storage on the watch, so your cards work when the phone is away.

Your control

Edit or delete cards in Zepp app › Profile › your watch › App settings › Zebrino. Switch suggestions off or reset them there. Backups exist only when you copy one yourself. Uninstalling clears the watch; delete your cards first to clear the Zepp app too.

Card numbers and children

The watch list shows only the last four digits of long numbers. Anyone with your unlocked watch could open your cards, like a wallet, so keep a passcode on. Zebrino collects no data from anyone, children included.

Changes and contact

Updates are published at zebrino.app/privacy. Questions: support@zebrino.app
