Zebrino Privacy Policy

Effective date: 10 October 2026

Zebrino puts your barcode cards on your Zepp OS watch. In short: your cards stay on your phone and your watch. No servers, no account, no ads, no tracking.

What Zebrino stores

  • Your cards: name, number, barcode type and value, colour, and the optional note, balance and expiry date you add.
  • Your settings: brightness, card order, suggestions on or off.
  • On the watch only: cards ready to display, recent cards and, with suggestions on, the times of day you usually show each card.

Zebrino does not collect your name, e-mail, location, contacts, health data or advertising IDs.

Where it is kept

On your phone, inside the Zepp app, and on your watch. Cards travel only between them, through the Zepp app. Zebrino never sends data to its developer or anyone else and does not use the internet. The Zepp app itself follows Zepp Health's privacy policy.

What Zebrino never does

No accounts, no ads, no analytics, no sale or sharing of data, no third-party SDKs. Its open-source libraries run on your devices and send nothing.

Camera scanning

The optional page zebrino.app/scan uses your camera only while you scan. Images are read on your phone, never uploaded or kept; scanned cards vanish with the page unless you copy them. It loads only its own files.

Permissions

Only local storage on the watch, so cards work when the phone is away.

Your control

Edit or delete cards in Zepp app › Profile › your watch › App settings › Zebrino, where suggestions can be turned off or reset. Backups exist only if you make one. Uninstalling clears the watch; delete your cards first to clear the Zepp app too.

Card numbers and children

The watch list shows only the last four digits of long numbers. Anyone with your unlocked watch could open your cards, like a wallet: use a passcode. Zebrino collects no data from anyone, children included.

Changes and contact

Updates are published at zebrino.app/privacy. Questions: support@zebrino.app
