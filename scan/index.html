Scan your cards with your phone

Point your phone's camera at a card's barcode or QR code, check what was read, then copy the result into Zebrino. The barcode is read here, on your phone: nothing is uploaded or kept.

Scanned cards

Give each card a name and check its number: the number printed on the card can differ from what its barcode holds.

    Add them to Zebrino

    1. Tap Copy for Zebrino.
    2. In the Zepp app, open Profile › your watch › App settings › Zebrino.
    3. Tap Import › Scan with your phone's camera, paste, check and import.

    Privacy

    The camera is used only while you scan, and stops as soon as a barcode is read. Images are read on your phone and never leave it. Scanned cards disappear when you close this page, unless you copy them. This page loads nothing but its own files. Privacy policy · Open-source licences
