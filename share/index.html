This link carries a card for Zebrino

The card's name, number and barcode are in the link itself. They stay on your phone: this page doesn't read them or send them anywhere.

Add it to your watch

  1. Copy the link (with everything after #).
  2. In the Zepp app, open Profile › your watch › App settings › Zebrino.
  3. Tap Import › Scan with your phone's camera, paste, check and import.

Want to add more cards? Scan them with your phone's camera.
